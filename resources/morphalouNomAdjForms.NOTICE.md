# morphalouNomAdjForms.tsv: attribution

`morphalouNomAdjForms.tsv` is a distillate (NOM and ADJ inflected spellings for the slots missing from
`LexiqueMixte.tsv`) of **Morphalou 3.1**, built by `python -m util.build_morphalou_forms`.

Morphalou 3.1 is a lexicon of French inflected forms produced by the ATILF laboratory (CNRS and
Université de Lorraine) and distributed by the CNRTL, under the licence **LGPL-LR** (Lesser General
Public License for Linguistic Resources). The distillate keeps only orthographic forms, grouped by lemma,
grammatical category, gender and number; it is redistributed under the same licence.

Citation: ATILF. *Morphalou 3.1.* ORTOLANG (Open Resources and TOols for LANGuage), https://www.ortolang.fr/market/lexicons/morphalou
(also https://www.cnrtl.fr/lexiques/morphalou/).
