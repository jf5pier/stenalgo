# Vowel-harmony candidates (harmonisation vocalique)

Findings of 2026-09-26, following `util/fixFirstSyllableE.py` and the design decision recorded
in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (decision 6: the harmony is ignored when that protects the lemma's
phonetics and reduces the stroke diversity needed to express its declension).

Generated by the read-only `python -m util.reportVowelHarmony` (writes
`scratch/vowel-harmony-report.tsv`, gitignored; rerun to regenerate). It compares each mid vowel
(E/e, O/o, 9/2) at the same position across a lemma's LexiqueMixte rows: 687 disagreements in 663
lemmas. Nothing in the lexicon was changed by it.

| Class | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `harmony` | 60 | tense rows all followed by a high/tense nucleus, lax rows not (the label is generous, see below) |
| `reverse` | 8 | the opposite; mostly `-eur`/`-euse` (`décodeur`/`décodeuse`); review by hand |
| `mixed` | 619 (`E/e` 90, `O/o` 527, `9/2` 2) | no clear signal; mostly coarse infinitive `e`/`o` against lax finite forms (`agresser` 15 lax / 1 tense, `atterrir` 20 / 1), the shape of the `essayer`/`descendre` cases already fixed |

Caveat: the closed/open-syllable filter that removes regular position alternations is only
partly reliable (a doubled consonant closes the syllable but the syllabification puts it in the
next onset), so `mixed` still holds regular alternations.

## The 60 `harmony` cases, read by hand

1. **Position alternation, not harmony (~40).** `-onner`/`-oter`/`-ationner` verbs (`abonner`,
   `boycotter`, `culotter`, `goudronner`, `rationner`, `positionner`, `pouponner`, `étalonner`,
   `désordonner`, `perquisitionner`…) and the `E/e` verbs `atterrer`, `paresser`,
   `désintéresser`. Lax vowel in the closed syllable (`abonne` /abOn/), tense before `-é`/`-er`
   (`abonner` /abone/). The "next vowel is `e`" signal is only the infinitive/participle ending.
   This is the regular loi de position the generator learns. OPEN DECISION: leave as is, or flatten
   to `O` in every form like `essayer`, which changes the strokes of ~40 lemmas.
2. **Genuine harmony between two mid vowels in one word (~12).** `cosmologique`, `radiologique`,
   `radioscopique`, `étiologique`, `troglodytique`, `rototo`, `coronarien`, `ovoïdal`, `philosophal`,
   `autographier`, `cochonnée`, `corroborer`, `lobotomiser`, `monopoliser`. The first `o` follows
   the next vowel (`monopolisé` `O` vs `monopolisa` `o`, 1 row against 11). Proposed: `O` in
   `-ologique`/`-oscopique`/`coronarien`/`philosophal`; the majority vowel `o` for `-oliser` and
   `corroborer`. This is the natural first batch.
3. **Suffix-driven alternation (~8).** `greffier`/`greffière`, `potier`/`potière`,
   `gondolier`/`gondolière`, `ferronnier`/`ferronnière`, `croqueur`/`croqueuse`,
   `roteur`/`roteuse`, `footballeur`/`footballeuse`: the masculine/feminine pair differs. Real French;
   leave alone unless one vowel per gender pair is wanted.
